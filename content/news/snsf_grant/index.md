The Swiss National Science Foundation has awarded MetaDiv: Ecological and Evolutionary Drivers of Phytochemical Diversity (Grant 215724) to <a href="/team/defossez-emmanuel/">Emmanuel Defossez</a> at the University of Neuchâtel. The CHF 381,293 project will run from 1 July 2023 to 30 June 2027.

MetaDiv will investigate why plants produce such diverse chemistry and how that diversity is shaped across species, environments and evolutionary lineages. The project will combine metabolomics with ecological, functional and phylogenetic information drawn from botanical garden collections and field research.

Its focus on plant chemical diversity is closely connected to DBGI's effort to build traceable, reusable links among living collections, biological metadata and metabolomics data. The work is based in the <a href="/labs/pgem/">Plant Eco-Metabolomics Group</a> at the University of Neuchâtel.

More information is available on the [SNSF grant page](https://data.snf.ch/grants/grant/215724).
