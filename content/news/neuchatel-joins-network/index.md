The <a href="/gardens/neuchatel/">Botanical Garden of Neuchâtel</a> has joined the DBGI network, formalising a long-standing collaboration with the <a href="/labs/pgem/">Plant Eco-Metabolomics Group</a> at the University of Neuchâtel.

The garden maintains eight botanical spaces, ranging from an evolution garden and medicinal-plant collections to an alpinum and greenhouses. This variety brings plants from contrasting taxonomic and environmental contexts within reach of a coordinated sampling campaign.

<a href="/team/mulhauser-blaise/">Blaise Mulhauser</a>, director of the garden, and <a href="/team/ruch-nicolas/">Nicolas Ruch</a>, its head curator, helped open the collections to the project and adapt DBGI procedures to the garden's established horticultural and documentation practices.

The first DBGI sampling campaign began on 9 May 2023, when members of the <a href="/labs/pgem/">Plant Eco-Metabolomics Group</a> brought the initiative's standardised collection and traceability workflows to the garden. The partnership supports research connecting botanical collections, ethnobotany, plant ecology and metabolomics.
