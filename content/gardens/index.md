The initiative has expanded to five botanical gardens across Europe: the Botanical Garden of the University of Fribourg, the Botanical Garden of the City of Neuchâtel, the Flore-Alpe Garden of Champex in Switzerland, Prague Botanical Garden in the Czech Republic, and the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew in the United Kingdom.
