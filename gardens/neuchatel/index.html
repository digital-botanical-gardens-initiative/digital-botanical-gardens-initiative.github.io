Botanical Garden of the City of Neuchâtel

Neuchâtel, Switzerland

The Botanical Garden of Neuchâtel contributes living collections, ethnobotanical expertise, and sampling workflows to DBGI.

The Botanical Garden of the City of Neuchâtel contributes living collections, ethnobotanical expertise, and garden workflows to DBGI. Its team supports sampling, collection curation, and the connection between botanical knowledge and open scientific reuse.
