The Digital BotanicalGardens Initiative

An open science effort to sample, measure, and digitise the chemistry of living botanical garden collections.

Botanical gardens maintain an extraordinary diversity of living plants. We use mass spectrometry to profile their chemistry and integrate all data into an open, searchable atlas of plant chemodiversity.

From living collections to reusable
knowledge

  1. Collect

    We use high-throughput workflows to sample botanical garden collections at scale and ensure each sampling record is accompanied by key specimen information and photographic records.

  2. Analyse

    Liquid chromatography–mass spectrometry acts as a digital scanner for plant chemistry. We profile each sample and process the resulting data into “chemical fingerprints”.

  3. Connect

    Chemical fingerprints and relevant specimen information are organised into a searchable atlas of plant chemistry for researchers, curators and the public.
