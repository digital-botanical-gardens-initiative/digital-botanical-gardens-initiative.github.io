What gardens can't reach, and why going further

Garden collections are unevenly distributed

Botanical gardens collections are unevenly distributed across the globe. Most sit in Europe and North America, while the biodiversity-rich regions (e.g., Africa and South America) remain far less covered.

Most species are still in the wild

Even taking into account all 1,100 living collections worldwide, more than 70% of plant diversity remains beyond the reach of botanical gardens.

Human preference shapes garden collections

It is known that humans are drawn to colourful, showy flowering plants7. Botanical gardens likewise tend to cultivate ornamental and traditionally used species. These choices can bias sampling towards particular regions and collection histories.

Botanical gardens are a starting point. Sampling wild populations and ecosystems is the goal of the Earth Metabolome Initiative.
