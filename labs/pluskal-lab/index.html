Pluskal Lab

IOCB Prague

Prague, Czech Republic

Visit lab website

The Pluskal Lab combines metabolomics, mass spectrometry, molecular networking, bioinformatics, and machine-learning approaches to study plant specialized metabolites. Within DBGI, the lab contributes analytical and computational expertise for profiling botanical collections.
